=== KEY ARCHITECTURAL FEATURES ===

✅ Source Tracking:  Every record tracks origin, external ID, URL, license
✅ Deduplication:    (source, sourceId) unique constraint prevents duplicates
✅ License Compliance:

✅ Error Handling:    Partial ingestion; errors logged per-record
✅ Job Tracking:      Audit trail of all ingestion runs
✅ Scheduled Runs:    Daily OSM, weekly Wikidata (extensible)
✅ Spatial Support:   PostGIS Point geometry + GIST indexes
